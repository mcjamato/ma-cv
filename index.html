01 — About

I work at the intersection of data analytics, software development, and education. My goal is to build tools that are technically sound, visually clear, and easy for real people to use.

My projects begin with a practical problem: organize scattered information, reveal useful trends, automate repetitive work, or make advanced technology easier to learn. I then design the data model, application workflow, interface, and reporting experience around that problem.

This portfolio highlights four products in development: a health and wellness analytics platform, an AI coding assistant, an AI sports highlight editor, and a business performance forecasting application.
